Catch to Cook

Sea fish, cleaned and ready for your kitchen.

Our story

Catch to Cook started with a simple idea: good sea fish should be easy to bring home. Cleaning, scaling and cutting a large fish takes time and skill, and not every kitchen has either. So we do that part for you. You choose the fish, we prepare it, and it reaches your door ready for the pan.

What we offer

  • Whole fish. Sold by the kilo, for those who like to cut it their own way.
  • Ready to Cook. Cleaned and cut portions of selected fish, priced per pack.
  • A wide range. From everyday catches at Tk 350 per kg to premium fish like Lakka and Red Coral, plus brown shrimp.

How it works

  1. Choose. Browse the price list and pick your fish.
  2. Message. Tap Order and send us the fish and quantity on WhatsApp.
  3. We prepare. Whole or Ready to Cook, as you prefer.
  4. Home delivery. Your order is delivered to your door.

Why Catch to Cook

  • Clear prices. Every fish is listed with its price per kg or per pack, so there are no surprises.
  • Less work. Ready to Cook saves you the cleaning and cutting.
  • Easy ordering. No app and no account, just WhatsApp.
